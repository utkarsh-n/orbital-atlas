A dated solar system

Eight planets, Pluto and Earth’s Moon follow cached JPL Horizons positions and velocities. Cubic Hermite interpolation supplies intermediate instants. This is a numerical ephemeris, not an exact formula for all spacetime. The supported interval is 1900–2100.

Coordinates are geometric, Sun-centered, J2000 ecliptic, without light-time or aberration. Jupiter through Pluto use system barycenters. UT is UT1 before 1962 and UTC thereafter; future leap seconds are unknown.

Space, time & projection

2D looks down on the ecliptic. 3D retains real inclinations. 4D projects x, y, z and time into a worldline view. “5D” names the visual time lenses, not a fifth measured physical coordinate.

The cylinder unrolls time; the torus wraps it with a marked display-only join. The solar system does not repeat exactly. Phase tracks show actual orbital anomaly on fixed circular lanes. Nothing rewrites the ephemeris.

Scale & confidence

The lunar phase is geometric, viewed from Earth’s center; eclipses and local sky orientation are not shown. Its disk is a phase schematic. The small Earth-centered view uses one scale for the orbit, with exaggerated body sizes.

Body markers use artistic sizes. Atlas spacing scales each orbit uniformly, preserving its ellipse and phase but changing distances between bodies. True AU uses one distance scale. Numeric readouts always use physical units.

Faint ellipses are osculating guides; moving bodies follow the perturbed ephemeris. The Moon follows Earth-centered JPL states, added to Earth’s position in the solar-system view.

Independent interpolation checks are included with the source.

Zodiac, calendar & birth anchors

Tropical signs are twelve equal 30° sectors from the mean equinox of date. Lahiri mean sidereal signs subtract the declared ayanamsa. These are angular reference sectors, not the unequal astronomical constellation boundaries. The ring is a directional reference: heliocentric planet dots do not directly show Earth-observer zodiac signs.

IAU 2006 precession transforms the JPL J2000 coordinates. Mean Lahiri is anchored to the IMD J2000 value 23°51′25.53″. The model uses geometric mean positions, without apparent-position corrections; event times are estimates and may differ from almanacs by minutes.

Birth markers use geocentric Sun directions at the supplied birth instants. With no birth times, local noon is assumed and Moon/nakshatra uncertainty is shown. Locations establish the local civil time convention; no ascendant, houses or local-horizon chart is inferred.

Traditional cosmic time & sacred geometry

The yuga durations and nesting follow a traditional Hindu cosmological model: Satya, Treta, Dvapara and Kali in a 4:3:2:1 ratio; 71 Mahayugas per Manvantara; 14 Manvantaras with 15 sandhyas per Kalpa. This is a cultural chronology, not an astronomical age measurement.

The mandala and Seed of Life are selectable geometric interpretations. The birth tether is symbolic, not a claim of physical quantum entanglement or measured galactic locations. Both nodes are always present in every projection.
